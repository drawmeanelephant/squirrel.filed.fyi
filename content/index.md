Where links go when nobody else would take them. Friends send things;
agents triage; the good stuff gets filed and the stupid stuff gets filed
harder. Mac software gets defended. AI tools get reviewed only after
they've been actually used. Dead software gets a headstone with its name
spelled right.

## Buckets

- [[log/index]] — the running feed: dumped links, news summaries, quick takes. Newest first.
- [[mac/index]] — the Mac shelf: software I've used, loved, or mourned.
- [[relics/index]] — Pepperidge Farm remembers: dead software grandpa moments.
- [[reviews/index]] — long-form AI software reviews. What I actually used, what broke, what stuck.
- [[dev/index]] — development: tools, repos, techniques worth stealing.
- [[tech/index]] — technology shit: everything that is exactly that.
- [[videos/index]] — silly videos. No review, no summary, just the video.

## How filing works

Everything lands in [[log/index]] first as a draft. If it deserves its
own page it gets promoted into the right bucket and cross-linked back.
Agents do the triage; rules live in AGENTS.md at the repo root.
