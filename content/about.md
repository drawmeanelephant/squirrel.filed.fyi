# About

I been shittin' since before you even farted.

I installed Mac OS X Public Beta in 2000. I was there when Finder had
brushed metal and the Dock was a novelty. I watched Steve Jobs demo
Exposé and thought "this is the future" — and I was right, for about
six years until every other operating system stole it. I've been using
Mac OS X through every name change, every controversial redesign, every
"courage" moment, and every time they moved where the scroll bars were.
I have opinions about Aqua. I have opinions about skeuomorphism. I have
opinions about whatever the hell they're calling the design language
this week.

I've been on the internet since before it was cool, which means I've
been on the internet since before it was uncool, which means I've been
on the internet through the entire cycle and I'm still here.

## What this site is

squirrel.filed.fyi is a filing cabinet for links, Mac software, AI
reviews, and dead software that deserves a headstone. Friends send
things; agents triage; the good stuff gets filed and the stupid stuff
gets filed harder.

It's built with [Boris](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/boris), a
Zig static-site compiler I made because the existing options were all
written in JavaScript and I wanted something that compiled to a single
binary and didn't need a runtime. The site runs on GitHub Pages. The
content lives in Markdown. The agents do the filing. I do the
editorializing.

## What I care about

**Mac software that respects your time.** I've been using Macs since
the 90s. I've seen apps come and go. The ones that stick are the ones
that understand you're trying to do something, not perform
productivity.

**Dead software that earned a eulogy.** Not everything deserves a
headstone, but the things that shaped how we use computers today
do. Even if nobody under 30 has heard of them.

**AI tools that actually work.** I'll review an AI tool after I've
used it long enough to have an opinion. Not after the press release.
Not after the demo. After it's broken something and I've had to fix
it.

**Technology that is exactly that.** Not "innovation." Not
"disruption." Not "the future of X." Just technology. Sometimes it's
good. Sometimes it's bad. Usually it's both.

## The name

squirrel.filed.fyi — because everything I find gets filed somewhere,
and I have the attention span of a squirrel on espresso. The .fyi
domain is doing a lot of heavy lifting. Yes, it's a real domain. No,
it doesn't stand for what you think it stands for.

## The projects

I build things in Zig because I like knowing what every byte is doing.
[DipshitOS](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/DipshitOS) is a
from-scratch AArch64 operating system that boots on Apple silicon.
[Boris](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/boris) is the static site
compiler that powers this site. The documentation for DipshitOS is
compiled by Boris. The documentation site for Boris is compiled by
Boris. It's Zig all the way down.

## On being opinionated

I build opinionated site publishing platforms. In tech, "opinionated"
means "we picked a frontmatter schema and a file structure and we're
not going to apologize for it." It means good organization and
reasonable defaults. It means YAML over JSON, nested folders over
dump-everything-in-one-directory, and God help you if you want to
rearrange the nav.

Actual opinionated means something different. It means saying
Netanyahu is a war criminal when the "opinionated" framework people
mean we want clean frontmatter. It means having a position on things
that matter instead of having a position on tab width. The entire tech
industry has watered down "opinionated" to mean "we have defaults"
while avoiding every actual opinion that would make someone
uncomfortable.

This site is opinionated in both senses. The frontmatter has exactly
seven keys. The filing rules are strict. The agents follow AGENTS.md.
And also: the Doomed AI series exists because someone needed to say
what everyone in the industry already knows but won't print. The
reviews section only publishes after I've actually used the thing. The
relics section exists because dead software deserves a headstone with
its name spelled right.

## The philosophy

Nothing is lost, nothing is created, everything is transformed.
— Antoine-Laurent de Lavoisier, 1789

I didn't pick that quote. A randomly generated workspace slug did.
But it turned out to be the perfect description of how I think about
building things: every byte accounted for, every system verified, every
claim grounded in something I can point to. No guesses presented as
results. No marketing voice. No filler.

Just links, software, and opinions — filed, not forgotten.
