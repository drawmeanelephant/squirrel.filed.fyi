# Pepperidge Farm remembers

This is the memberberries shelf: software that is long dead, not
especially relevant to today's ecosystem, and remembered by roughly no
one — which is exactly why it gets a page. Everyone remembers DOS.
Nobody remembers the stuff below unless they lived in it.

Different from [[mac/index]], which holds things you could still argue
about. Everything here is a closed casket.

## In the ground

- [[relics/unsanity]] — Unsanity. The haxie empire. Your Mac used to have a personality because a company sold you one in 400KB.
- [[relics/virtual-pc]] — Connectix Virtual PC, later Microsoft Virtual PC. An entire Pentium, faked convincingly, on a G4.
