# Spell — absorbed, not imploded

- Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/16/reddit-spell-machine-learning/) · [spell.ml](https://spell.ml/) (mined from the owner's backlog, 2026-08-26)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: every doomed-AI collection needs its control group — the company
  that didn't explode, just got eaten. Spell ran ML experiments on
  rented GPUs so you didn't need your own rack, founded by Serkan
  Piantino out of the Facebook News Feed and FAIR lineage. Decent
  pedigree, real product, and then the MLOps field got crowded by
  hyperscalers giving it away plus a dozen funded startups fighting
  over the remainder.

Reddit acquired it in June 2022 for an undisclosed sum and shut new
commercial signups mid-acquisition — the product died politely,
in advance. The team folded into Reddit's ads, safety, and relevance
machine learning; Piantino became VP of Foundational Product. For a
while the homepage stayed up as a tombstone reading only "Acquired by
Reddit, Inc. in June 2022." As of this filing the domain itself has
lapsed into a GoDaddy for-sale page: the grave is on the market too.

A postscript the mining process supplied free of charge: the
conversation that surfaced this story took place in 2026, and the AI
assistant conducting it described the June 2022 acquisition —
announced in an official blog post, covered by every tech outlet — as
"sketchy and unverified," terms "sketchy," details unconfirmed. Four
years of training-data lag delivered with total confidence as due
diligence. The human in the thread was more current than the machine
summarizing the world for him.

The series pattern so far: [[log/2026-08-26-meta-project-ot|Meta]] is
what doom looks like when the documents leak. Spell is what it looks
like when they don't — no implosion, no revolt, just a talent
acquisition wearing a product's name. Most companies in this graveyard
don't crash; they get hired.
