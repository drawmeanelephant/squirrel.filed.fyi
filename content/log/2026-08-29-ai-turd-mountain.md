# AI Has Arrived in American Life as a Giant Turd Mountain

- Source: https://www.doomsdayscenario.co/p/ai-is-making-everything-worse (found 2026-08-29)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: Garrett Graff's August 2026 essay is the single best articulation of why AI discourse is so poisonous right now. His central metaphor is devastating: AI arrived as "a giant turd mountain" — no one wants it, it makes everything worse when it arrives, and it's been pushed by "the absolute worst people in modern American life." He compares it to if, after inventing electricity, every factory worker was assigned to start each workday by receiving an electric shock. The essay catalogs the crimes: pillaging creative work, hallucinating wrong answers 45% of the time, failing to deliver the promised 10X productivity (turns out it's more like an hour a week), and for every useful output generating hours of slop a coworker has to deal with. Meanwhile, AI boosters are confused why the public isn't grateful, comparing themselves to Robert Moses wondering why New Yorkers don't appreciate him. Students booed AI at commencement ceremonies. Commencement! The one event where you're legally required to be optimistic.
