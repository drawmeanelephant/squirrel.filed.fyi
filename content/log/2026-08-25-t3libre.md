## What it is

[T3Libre](https://github.com/maria-rcks/t3libre) is a fork of T3 Code —
the open-source control surface for coding agents (web, desktop, mobile)
from the Ping.gg/T3 ecosystem — "liberated" on one principle: *open
source software should never, under any circumstances, lead to a
sustainable business.* Commitments: no customers, no business model, no
product strategy, every PR accepted on moral grounds, money thrown into
the pit forever. The icons are light now. The revolution is complete.

## The read

It's a precision parody of the righteous liberation fork — the
OpenTofu/Valkey/OpenSearch genre where the community forks a project to
save it from its vendor. Applied here to a project that is already MIT
and not going anywhere, by someone who (per the owner, unverified) works
on or near T3 itself. A liberation fork from inside the castle is either
the healthiest possible sign about a project's culture or the funniest
possible sign about its community, and the correct answer is yes.
