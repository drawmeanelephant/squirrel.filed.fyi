# Altman Admits People Hate Data Centers as His Data Center Guy Quits

- Source: https://fortune.com/2026/08/27/sam-altman-openai-data-center-backlash/ (found 2026-08-29)
- Source: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openais-head-of-data-centers-has-left-company-6d24fd83 (found 2026-08-29)
- Source: https://www.businessinsider.com/sam-altman-data-centers-unpopularity-views-2026-8 (found 2026-08-29)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: The timing is exquisite. Sam Altman told Time "Clearly, people hate data centers — right now, at least" in the same week his head of data centers, Chris Malone, left the company. Malone joined in March 2025, right when Stargate launched, and bailed after less than a year and a half. Meanwhile: 7 in 10 Americans oppose data centers nearby (Gallup), 48 projects representing $156B have been blocked or stalled by local resistance, 120+ moratoriums proposed in 38 states, Texas Governor Abbott ordered regulators to scrutinize data center grid connections, Pennsylvania's Governor Shapiro removed AI data centers from fast-track permitting, and 60% of Republicans and 53% of MAGA voters oppose them (Fox News poll). The bipartisan data center hate coalition is the most unified thing in American politics right now. OpenAI says it still doesn't have enough compute and is planning beyond Stargate's original 10GW target. The phrase "If anything, we should have bought a lot more" from an OpenAI exec in the same article where 70% of Americans say "not in my backyard" is the most tone-deaf energy in tech since "let them eat inference."
