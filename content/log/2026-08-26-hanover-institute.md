# The Hanover Institute does not exist

- Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/26/fake-thinktank-israel-ai-propaganda) · [the institute](https://hanoverinstitute.com) · [FARA filings](https://efile.fara.gov/ords/fara/f?p=1381:200:::NO:RP,200:P200_REG_NUMBER:7732) (sent by the owner, 2026-08-26)
- Verdict: promote-to-tech
- Why: the purest expression yet of the era's core delusion — that any
  problem, up to and including geopolitics, is a search-engine-
  optimization problem. A pro-Israel messaging site badged as the
  "Hanover Institute for Public Policy" published 124 reports totaling
  over half a million words in nine days, every headline shaped like a
  chatbot query, sourced punctiliously enough to masquerade as
  academic. No legal entity, no address, no staff, no bylines. Its own
  terms of service are governed by "the state in which the Institute
  is established," which they decline to name.

## The machine-fed-to-the-machine details

The llms.txt file shipped with the site initially belonged to Res,
an "AI-native content platform" whose product is getting your content
cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. The Guardian
archived it before someone noticed. One 2022 study of Facebook
comments appears 454 times across 88 reports — including entries on
Ottoman-era land registration. The funding page concedes, in a
sentence for the anthologies: "An earlier version of this page said
the Institute operated without external funding, and that was true
when it was written."

Downstream, the chain runs: Israeli government agency LaPam → Havas
Media Germany → Brad Parscale's Clock Tower X ($15M) and friends,
whose previous sites now sit in Common Crawl's index 294 times —
which is to say, inside the training data of the next generation of
models. The chatbots of 2027 are currently being assigned the
reading.

## The read

Here's the doomed-AI rhyme: it worked, and changed nothing. ChatGPT
now cites the Hanover Institute — while flagging, in the same
breath, the controversy over who funds it and why it exists. The
models were successfully groomed; the humans were not touched. Gallup
shows American sympathy flipping Palestinian for the first time;
Israeli officials brief their own press that the campaign flopped —
"we paid a lot of money, but the situation only got worse" — with one
calling it "a terrible hoax on the scale of Pollard" and asking, of
Parscale specifically, "what did he do with it?"

Same shape as
[[log/2026-08-26-meta-project-ot|Project OT]]'s +220% code churn
resolving to +36% features: enormous synthetic activity, zero movement
in the physical world. The machines can now be persuaded of anything.
Unfortunately for everyone financing this, the machines were never
against you.

a practice: "Whether it's foreign governments, whether it's
companies, whether it's even just a guy who owns an ice cream shop —
it's part of the new reality that we live in."
