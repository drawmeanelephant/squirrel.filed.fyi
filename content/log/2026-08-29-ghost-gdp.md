# Ghost GDP: The Economy That Benefits No One You Know

**Source:** [The week the AI scare turned real](https://fortune.com/2026/02/28/ai-scare-trade-mass-layoffs-white-collar-recession-citrini-shumer-viral-doomsday-essays/) — Fortune (February 28, 2026)

**Filed to:** [[tech/ghost-gdp]]

## The concept

"Ghost GDP" — coined by Citrini Research in a Substack essay viewed 85 million times on X — describes economic output that generates value for compute owners but never circulates through the human consumer economy. GDP goes up. Wages go down. The line on the chart says everything is fine. The person who used to earn the money that line represents is folding shirts.

## The timeline

- **Tuesday:** Citrini posts their doomsday essay about a "human intelligence displacement spiral"
- **Monday:** The Dow drops 800 points
- **Thursday:** Jack Dorsey announces Block will lay off 40% of staff because "intelligence tools have changed what it means to build and run a company"
- **Friday:** Block stock rises 14%

## The human cost

The article quotes a 42-year-old former Snapchat content executive who hasn't found full-time work since 2023. She's working retail now. She said showing up to her first day and putting on a name tag was "very shocking. Like I just fell off a cliff and I have no flashlight."

## Wall Street's response

Morgan Stanley's official response? The future will have exciting new jobs like "vibe coding" and "Chief AI Officer." Citadel Securities published a blistering takedown arguing the economics are unsound. Meanwhile, 99% of corporate executives think AI will cause layoffs at their companies in the next two years.

**Verdict:** Ghost GDP is the term that finally names what everyone vaguely felt but couldn't articulate: the economy can grow while your life gets worse, and AI is the accelerant.
