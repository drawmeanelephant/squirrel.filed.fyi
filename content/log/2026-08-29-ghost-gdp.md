# Ghost GDP: The Economy That Benefits No One You Know

- Source: https://fortune.com/2026/02/28/ai-scare-trade-mass-layoffs-white-collar-recession-citrini-shumer-viral-doomsday-essays/ (found 2026-08-29)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: The concept of "Ghost GDP" — economic activity that generates value for compute owners but never circulates through the human consumer economy — is the most depressingly elegant economic term of the decade. Citrini Research posted their doomsday essay and the Dow dropped 800 points the next Monday. Jack Dorsey then announced Block would lay off 40% of staff because "intelligence tools have changed what it means to build and run a company." Block stock rose 14%. The article quotes a 42-year-old former Snapchat executive now working retail: "I just fell off a cliff and I have no flashlight." Morgan Stanley's official response? The future will have exciting new jobs like "vibe coding" and "Chief AI Officer." Sure. Meanwhile, 99% of corporate executives think AI will cause layoffs at their companies in the next two years, which is roughly the same unanimity rate as "the sky is blue."
