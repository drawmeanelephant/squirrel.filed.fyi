# Freebuff

## What it is

Five free AI products from CodebuffAI — Desktop, CLI, Web, Cloud, and
Chat — no subscription, no credits, no API key. Text ads pay for the
models. Under the hood it's the open Codebuff multi-agent framework:
specialized agents for file-finding, implementation, review, research,
and browser use instead of one model and one prompt doing everything.
Apache-2.0 TypeScript monorepo, 10.8k stars, ~8.8k commits — actively
shipped, not abandonware.

## The catalog

Curated model picker rather than bring-your-own-key: GPT-5.6 Luna,
DeepSeek V4 Flash and Pro, MiMo 2.5, Ox Alpha (1M context, unmetered,
flagged experimental), GLM 5.2 via earned sessions, Gemini 3.1 Flash
Lite quietly powering specialist tasks. Per-model caps were replaced by
shared daily sessions; regional limited-access tiers exist.

## The trade, stated out loud

Credit where due: the README tells you the deal before you take it.
Prompts and code may be analyzed to personalize ads, data may train
models where flagged, and outside opt-out jurisdictions "this processing
may be required to use the free service." Ads-for-inference is an
honest business model compared to most, but it's still reading your
code for grocery coupons — fine for hobby repos, think twice before
pointing it at anything sensitive.
