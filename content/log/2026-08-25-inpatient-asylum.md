## The rabbit hole

**Inpatient** is the collaborative project of [Ren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren_(musician))
— the Welsh artist who went viral with "Hi Ren," a two-selves rap battle
against his own brain, and who does everything independent via his Sick
Boi label — and [Chris Webby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Webby),
the Connecticut battle-rap-adjacent weed-rap lifer. Production from JP
On Da Track. Released July 22, 2026 on Sick Boi / EightyHD.

What makes it worth the two hours: it refuses to sit still. Old-school
boom-bap into ska-flecked punk into storytelling pop, with running
characters, skits, and a monster-in-the-ward narrative arc ([RockSound's
writeup](https://rocksound.tv/news/ren-and-chris-webby-release-collaborative-album-asylum)
calls it "the sort of record that defines a time and a place"). Webby
matches Ren bar for bar, which nobody predicted and everybody involved
seems a little surprised by.

Chart receipts, because independent matters:
[#3 UK Official Downloads](https://www.officialcharts.com/albums/reninpatientchris-webby-asylum/),
#9 Independent Albums, #38 Album Sales. No label machinery. Lyrics and
tracklist at [Genius](https://genius.com/albums/Inpatient-ren-and-chris-webby/Asylum),
streaming on [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/asylum/6793326451).
