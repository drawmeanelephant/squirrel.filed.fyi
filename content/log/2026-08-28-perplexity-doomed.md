# Perplexity AI — Voted "Most Likely to Fail" and Facing Existential Legal Risk

- Sources: [TSG Invest](https://tsginvest.com/perplexity-ai/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/legalindustry/is-perplexitys-no-volition-copyright-strategy-clever-legal-shield-or-long-shot--pracin-2026-03-11/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/amazon-wins-order-blocking-access-perplexitys-ai-shopping-agent-2026-03-10/)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: The industry already voted on this one.

## The L-takes

**The industry verdict.** At industry events, Perplexity was voted "most likely to fail" by founders and investors. That's not a blog post take — that's the people who fund startups telling you they don't see it.

**The valuation.** ~$20B private valuation after multiple rounds, $1.5B+ raised from Accel, SoftBank, Nvidia, Bezos, IVP. Valuations ballooned from hundreds of millions to tens of billions in under 2 years. Classic startup hypergrowth pattern that doesn't necessarily reflect profits or unit economics.

**The legal wall.** NYT, Chicago Tribune, and others have sued over unauthorized use and summarization of content. Perplexity's defense — claiming it doesn't have "volition" — is viewed as a long shot. A federal judge also issued a preliminary injunction blocking Perplexity's AI from accessing Amazon accounts, calling the behavior unauthorized. These aren't noise — they could curb core product features.

**The margins.** Infrastructure cost per query vs. revenue per query suggests margins are thin or negative, especially on free users. Revenue sharing with publishers is a cost, not a moat. Reddit users report marketing spend and feature development have slowed — classic cash crunch signals.

**The competition.** Google (Gemini/AI search), OpenAI (ChatGPT + search), Microsoft, Anthropic — all with cash reserves, distribution channels, and user bases orders of magnitude larger. Unless Perplexity finds a real niche or huge product differentiation, it's easy to see being squeezed out or commoditized.

**The verdict:** "A classic VC hype bubble with real tech but weak defensibility."
