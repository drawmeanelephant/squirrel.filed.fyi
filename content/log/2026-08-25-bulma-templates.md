# Bulma templates

- Source: [BulmaTemplates/bulma-templates](https://github.com/BulmaTemplates/bulma-templates) (sent by the owner, 2026-08-25)
- Verdict: toss
- Why: the internet's most-recommended pile of flexbox starter pages,
  and by all accounts fine ones — a decade of free templates from
  Bulma 0.1.1 to 1.0.x. But a template gallery for someone else's CSS
  framework is precisely the genre of thing this site exists to not
  use: boris compiles the graph, the lab theme is hand-rolled, and no
  `div class="column is-three-quarters"` shall enter this house.

**Disclosure, entered into the record:** boris's own `columns` theme
names its custom properties `--bulma-primary`, `--bulma-ink`, and
thirty-five siblings — an homage its README defends with the words
"Not Bulma." The court accepts that the house remains div-free and
rules the variable names a misdemeanorable accent. The toss stands;
the halo does not.
