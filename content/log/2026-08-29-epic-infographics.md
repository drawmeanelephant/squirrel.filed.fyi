# Epic Infographics

- Source: https://github.com/OrRon/EpicInfographics (sent by user, 2026-08-29)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: A well-built skill for AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, etc.) that generates infographics with actual design intent — scene-native composition, computed data-viz geometry, color-blind-safe palettes, and a mandatory render-review anti-slop checklist. Uses Playwright + Chromium to render HTML/CSS to retina PNGs. Gallery of 13 examples across 12 design languages (blueprint, dark-glass, naturalist-plate, isometric-world, etc.). The "no Tailwind blue" philosophy is refreshing. Could be useful for the site's own visual content down the road.
