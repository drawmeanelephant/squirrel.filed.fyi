# San Francisco Now Has Nihilistic Advertising, and Everyone Just Accepts It

- Source: https://sfstandard.com/pacific-standard-time/2026/08/07/sf-ai-billboards-dystopian-not-funny/ (found 2026-08-29)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: San Francisco's AI billboards have evolved past marketing into something weirder — slogans like "Own your inference" and "Your agents need agents!" that don't sell anything anyone can buy. The SF Standard's Emily Dreyfuss argues they're doing real psychological damage to the city: a constant, ambient reminder that a conversation is happening and you're not part of it. New Yorkers defaced the "Friend" AI necklace ads within days. San Franciscans just... stared. One resident is fighting back by paying $6,000/month for a billboard that roasts the AI billboards. Peak "this is fine" energy from a city that used to mean something.
