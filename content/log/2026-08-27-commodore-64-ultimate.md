# Commodore 64 Ultimate — Cyberpunk 2077 Edition

- Source: https://commodore.net/computer/?sku=c64cu (main product page) + https://commodore.net/store/commodore64/?shape=Breadbin&color=Cyberpunk+2077 (Cyberpunk variant)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: Commodore is selling new C64 hardware built on AMD Xilinx Artix-7 FPGA, not emulation. Original 1986 injection-molded case tooling for the C64C variant, dual SID chip sockets with auto-detection, 128MB RAM, HDMI 1080p, USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi. The Cyberpunk 2077 Breadbin is a specific colorway — translucent dark with neon accents. Ships with 100+ games on a USB "cassette", GEOS, demos, and music. Pre-orders from $299.99, Batch 3 units still available. Real hardware, real nostalgia, real goosebumps.
