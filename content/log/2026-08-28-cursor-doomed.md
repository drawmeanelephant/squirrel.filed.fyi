# Cursor's $29B Valuation and the AI Coding Tool That Can't Survive Without VC Money

- Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/cursor-raises-23b-in-series-d-funding-at-293b-valuation-93CH-4356301), [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/cursor/), [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/lovable-growth-exec-openai-anthropic-competition-cursor-replit-vibe-coding-2026-3)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: The numbers are real but the business model is VC-fueled rocket fuel, not revenue.

## The L-takes

**The valuation.** Cursor (Anysphere) raised a $2.3B Series D at ~$29.3B valuation. Investors include Accel, a16z, Coatue, Nvidia, Google, DST, and Thrive. That's on par with some of the biggest AI infra plays. They can burn a lot of cash without going bankrupt — for now.

**The revenue.** Annualized revenue reportedly over $1B, potentially ~$2B. That's not "startup with no customers" — those are big SaaS numbers. Enterprise contracts are where the money is. 60%+ enterprise revenue per reports.

**The burn.** Here's where it falls apart. AI tools have massive infrastructure costs — compute, models, engineers. Cursor likely burns close to or exceeding their revenue on growth and infrastructure. The $2.3B Series D was basically a giant fuel injection to keep them running. Without it, the whole thing stalls.

**The user's realization:** "I'm not seeing where they are making money without outside investment." That's the whole story. Revenue covers part of the costs, but not enough to fund growth without dumping hundreds of millions from investors. They're a supercharged rocket powered by VC money, not self-sustaining revenue.

**ChatGPT's hallucination.** During the analysis, ChatGPT claimed OpenAI has a "monopoly on a lot of LLM infrastructure." The user immediately called it out — OpenAI doesn't own infrastructure, they rent from Microsoft Azure and Oracle. Other players (AWS, Google, Meta) all have their own massive AI infrastructure. ChatGPT admitted the statement was "flat-out wrong" and "marketing-speak masquerading as fact." The user's response: "you're a fucking bullshitter thanks for nothing" and "no you're fucking not trustworthy."

**The competitive squeeze.** GitHub Copilot (~40-45% market share) has Microsoft's virtually unlimited deep pockets. Claude Code has Anthropic. IDE vendors are building built-in AI tools. Cursor's pricing advantage disappears when giants with deeper pockets start giving it away.
