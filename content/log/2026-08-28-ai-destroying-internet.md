# AI Job Loss Research Ignores How AI Is Utterly Destroying the Internet

- Source: [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-job-loss-research-ignores-how-ai-is-utterly-destroying-the-internet/)
- Verdict: keep
- Why: The research focuses on whether AI takes your job while ignoring that AI is already taking the internet itself.

## The L-takes

**The blind spot.** Much of the research on AI's impact on jobs, including studies by Anthropic, focuses on high-minded, work-related applications. It completely ignores the real ways AI is being used: generating porn, spam, fake content, and deepfake material at scale.

**The damage.** These uses are widespread and actively harming the internet ecosystem. Content discoverability is collapsing. Legitimate creators get buried under bot-generated noise. Algorithms can't tell real from AI-made. Social media feels less like a community and more like a junkyard.

**The research gap.** The critique isn't that job-loss research is wrong — it's that it's incomplete. It measures the thing that's easy to measure (jobs) while ignoring the thing that's happening everywhere (internet quality collapse). Small businesses, creators, and online industries are being harmed by AI spam in ways that don't show up in employment statistics.

**The L:** Everyone's worried about AI taking their job. Nobody's measuring AI taking the internet itself. The research papers ask "will AI replace you?" while AI is already replacing the platform you work on — flooding feeds with garbage, burying legitimate content, and destroying the trust that made the internet functional. The job loss is a rounding error compared to the ecosystem collapse.
