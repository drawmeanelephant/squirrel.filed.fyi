# Doomed AI

The red bucket. A running catalogue of AI transformations that were
doomed on arrival: companies replacing humans with agents before
checking whether agents can human, productivity miracles that turn out
to be typing, and the three-to-six-month delivery windows in which all
failed predictions get rescheduled.

Ground rules, learned from the material itself: consent matters more
than capability claims, internal metrics beat press releases, and
nobody has ever been saved by calling it an Organization
Transformation.

## The series so far

- [[log/2026-08-26-meta-project-ot]] — Reuters gets Meta's internal
  docs: 60% team-cut scenarios in two waves, +220% code churn for
  +36% shipped features, and a CEO who called off wave two hours
  before wave one when the spreadsheet finally admitted the agents
  couldn't work.

More where that came from. The bucket has room.
