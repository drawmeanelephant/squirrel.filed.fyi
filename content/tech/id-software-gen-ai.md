# id's Former Co-Owner Says They'd Absolutely Have Used Gen AI Back in the Day

Filed from [[log/2026-08-30-id-software-gen-ai]].

## The quote

Tim Willits — who ran id Software through the Quake-and-beyond era as
studio director and co-owner, now at Saber Interactive — told
[PC Gamer at Gamescom 2026](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/game-development/id-softwares-former-co-owner-says-they-absolutely-wouldve-used-gen-ai-back-in-the-day-john-carmack-would-be-like-making-skynet-or-something/)
that id absolutely would've used gen AI back in the day. id's whole
thing was adopting whatever new tech was fastest, so AI to generate
assets and fill the world would've been a no-brainer. And the line
everyone grabbed: *"John Carmack would be like making Skynet or
something."*

## The defense

It's the standard-issue veteran move: every old dev says they'd have
used the new thing if they'd had it. And it's true! id in the '90s was
a technology-maximalist shop — new rendering technique drops, id ships
it before the whitepaper cools. Of course they'd have used procedural
generation on steroids. The quote is honest about what id *was*.

It's also exactly what every studio says right before the asset
pipelines get gutted. "We would've used it too" is doing double duty:
nostalgia for the speaker, air cover for the layoffs. The people saying
it are never the ones whose jobs get automated by it.

## The footnote nobody dwells on

The guy whose name gets attached to "we all would've done it" is
Carmack — the person people point to as proof that technical maximalism
is good. But the actual Carmack mostly wants nothing to do with this
particular gold rush. The mascot for the defense doesn't endorse the
defense. That's the whole story in one sentence, really: the industry
keeps drafting Carmack's ghost to bless a future he'd find embarrassing.

## Verdict

Fun quote, true quote, and a perfect little artifact of 2026: the old
gods get invoked to sanctify the new slop pipeline, while the actual
old god is off doing something else. Skynet joke's good though.
