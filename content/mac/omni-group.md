# The Omni Group

[The Omni Group](https://www.omnigroup.com/) has been shipping Mac
software since the copyright line says 1994 — which makes them older
than most tech companies' *current pivot*. They started in NeXTSTEP
land, rode every transition since (OS X, Intel, App Store, Apple
Silicon, subscriptions), and are one of the few independent shops from
that era still alive, still private, and still selling you software
instead of a relationship with their investors.

## The suite

- **[OmniOutliner](https://www.omnigroup.com/omnioutliner)** — the
  elder statesman. Outlining done so well it shaped how a generation of
  Mac users thinks about hierarchical anything.
- **[OmniGraffle](https://www.omnigroup.com/omnigraffle)** — diagramming
  and rapid prototyping. What Visio would be if Visio were pleasant.
- **[OmniPlan](https://www.omnigroup.com/omniplan)** — project
  management with Gantt charts and resource leveling.
- **[OmniFocus](https://www.omnigroup.com/omnifocus)** — GTD-based task
  management; was there at the App Store's birth in 2008.
- **[Omni Labs](https://www.omnigroup.com/more)** — the retirement home:
  OmniDiskSweeper, OmniPresence, and [[mac/omniweb]].

## Why they matter on this shelf

Two things set Omni apart from the general run of software companies:

1. **They kept perpetual licenses.** When the industry converted to
   subscriptions because recurring revenue looks better in a slide deck,
   Omni added subscriptions without taking away the option to just buy
   the thing. This should not be remarkable. It is remarkable.
2. **They maintain their dead.** Discontinued products don't vanish;
   they move to Omni Labs with download links per macOS version going
   back to Tiger. Try getting that from a company that calls shutdown
   "sunsetting your journey."
