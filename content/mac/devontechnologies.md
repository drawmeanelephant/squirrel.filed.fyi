# DEVONtechnologies

[DEVONtechnologies](https://www.devontechnologies.com/) is a German Mac
shop that has been making information-management software since the
early 2000s. Their homepage asks "Would Umberto Eco have bought a Mac
for this?", which tells you exactly who they think their customer is:
someone with more documents than filing system.

## What their shit actually is

- **[DEVONthink](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonthink)** —
  the flagship. A local-first database for documents: PDFs, notes, web
  archives, mail, whatever you throw in. Two features made its name:
  automatic classification (it learns which folder a document belongs
  in) and "See Also," which surfaces related documents by content, not
  filename.
- **[DEVONthink To Go](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonthinktogo)** —
  iOS companion with sync.
- **[DEVONagent](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonagent)** —
  meta-search that runs your query across multiple engines and clusters
  the results. Pre-dates every "AI research assistant" by a decade.
- **[DEVONsphere Express](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonsphere)** —
  the "discover hidden connections" sidekick.
- **[Needful Things](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/freeware)** —
  their freebies shelf.

## The part that matters

The joke about DEVONthink is that it was doing AI before AI was cool,
except it isn't a joke: classification and See Also have been content-
analysis features running locally for twenty years — no subscription, no
cloud round-trip, no model card, no apology. In 2026 that same category
of feature gets you a $200M seed round and a waitlist. Here it's been a
checkbox in a document manager since roughly the Bush administration.

This page is a stub until real usage evidence lands in it — the plan is
a proper review once the current DEVONthink setup has enough mileage to
have opinions about sync, OCR, and whether See Also still beats grep.
