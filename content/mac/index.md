# The Mac shelf

Twenty-five-odd years of Mac software that was actually good. Some of it
is still shipping, some is a headstone with a download link. All of it
earned shelf space by being used for real.

## On the shelf

- [[mac/default-folder-x]] — 25-year review. Fixes the worst dialog on your computer.
- [[mac/devontechnologies]] — DEVONthink and friends. Local AI before AI was a valuation.
- [[mac/omni-group]] — The Omni Group. Thirty years of Cocoa productivity.
- [[mac/omniweb]] — the dead one. Browser, pioneer, gone. Deserved better.

## Filing rule

Mac software writeups live here instead of [[reviews/index]] unless
they're specifically about AI tooling. Same review structure applies;
usage evidence required either way.

Fully dead stuff that nobody argues about anymore goes down the hall to
[[relics/index]] instead. This shelf still has opinions; that one has
headstones.
